# TermJobs — Project Abstract

TermJobs is an AI-assisted contract hiring and workforce management platform designed to connect organizations, hiring managers, candidates, and contract professionals through a unified digital workflow. Traditional hiring processes often rely on disconnected spreadsheets, email conversations, and separate applications, making it difficult to track requirements, coordinate evaluations, and manage the transition from candidate selection to active employment engagements. TermJobs addresses these challenges by bringing recruitment and ongoing workforce administration into a shared platform with structured processes and clear responsibility at each stage.

The platform supports the recruitment lifecycle, beginning with the creation of hiring requisitions that capture skills, experience, location, engagement duration, and other role requirements. Hiring managers can prepare these requirements through structured forms or conversational AI assistance before submitting them for review and approval. Approved roles can be published for candidates to discover and apply. Resume parsing and AI-assisted candidate matching help organize applicant information and provide insights into alignment with role requirements. These capabilities support human evaluation, while authorized reviewers retain responsibility for screening, selection, and approval decisions.

TermJobs also provides tools for coordinating interview rounds, scheduling sessions, and recording evaluations. Dedicated workspaces help hiring managers and interviewers access relevant candidate information and follow application progress. After selection, onboarding workflows guide candidates through required information and checklist completion, helping teams track readiness for an engagement. Contract professionals can then use their portal to access assignment information, submit timesheets and expense claims, and report onboarding or work-related issues. Managers can review these submissions through the corresponding approval workflows.

Role-based access, in-app notifications, and connected records help participants understand their responsibilities and identify pending actions. By maintaining continuity between hiring requirements, candidate assessments, onboarding, and workforce activity, the platform aims to reduce repetitive administrative work and improve visibility across the engagement lifecycle. TermJobs combines AI assistance with human oversight to support a more coordinated, transparent, and accountable approach to contract hiring and workforce management.

**Keywords:** Contract hiring, workforce management, AI-assisted recruitment, requisition management, candidate screening, interview coordination, onboarding, timesheet management.
