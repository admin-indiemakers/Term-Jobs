# About TermJobs

> Content brief and draft copy for the TermJobs About page. Based on the product documentation and application modules in this repository. Proposed mission and vision wording below is editorial copy, rather than a statement of company history.

## 1. What is TermJobs?

**TermJobs is an AI-assisted contract hiring and workforce management platform that connects companies, hiring managers, staffing partners, candidates and contract professionals in one shared workflow.**

The platform brings together the work that happens before and after a hire: defining requirements, securing approvals, sourcing candidates, reviewing applications, scheduling interviews, managing onboarding, tracking work and preparing vendor billing.

Its focus is on making contract hiring easier to coordinate. Instead of managing each stage through separate spreadsheets, email threads and disconnected tools, teams can follow a requisition and its associated candidates through dedicated workspaces.

TermJobs combines conversational assistance with structured forms, human review and role-based approvals. AI helps teams prepare and interpret information; hiring managers, interviewers, Directors and other authorized reviewers remain responsible for their decisions.

**Suggested positioning statement:**

> From hiring requirements to active engagements, TermJobs helps teams manage contract talent in one connected platform.

## 2. Why TermJobs exists

Contract hiring involves more than finding a candidate. A company must explain its requirements, obtain approval, coordinate suppliers, evaluate applicants, arrange interviews and prepare a selected professional to begin work. Once an engagement starts, hours, expenses and billing also need attention.

When these activities happen in separate systems, teams can struggle to answer basic questions:

- Who submitted this requirement, and who needs to approve it?
- Which candidates are ready for review?
- What is the next interview or onboarding step?
- Which timesheets or expenses are awaiting approval?
- What information supports a vendor’s billing request?

TermJobs is designed to connect those activities and make responsibility, status and next steps easier to understand.

### Vision

A connected contract workforce ecosystem where requirements, talent evaluation, approvals and engagement operations move through a clear, shared process.

### Product principles

- **Connected work:** Keep hiring and engagement information linked across stages.
- **Clear ownership:** Make the submitting manager, reviewer and responsible team identifiable.
- **Human accountability:** Support decisions with AI assistance and structured information while retaining human review.
- **Practical visibility:** Give each participant access to the information and actions relevant to their role.
- **Continuity after selection:** Carry the workflow into onboarding, work tracking and billing.

## 5. How the platform works

### Step 1: Define the requirement

A Hiring Manager describes the role through a structured requisition form or conversational assistant. Requirements can include skills, experience, location, engagement duration, compensation information and application deadlines.

AI-assisted drafting helps turn those requirements into a structured role and job description that the manager can review and correct.

### Step 2: Obtain approval

The requisition moves into the company’s approval workflow. A Director can review the request, see the submitting Hiring Manager and approve it or request revisions.

When revisions are required, the manager can update the details and resubmit the requisition. Deadline checks help keep expired requirements out of active approval and application flows until appropriately corrected.

### Step 3: Reach potential candidates

Approved and published roles can be made available through supported public application and staffing-partner workflows. Recruiters submit profiles, while applicants can apply directly through the public Open Roles experience.

### Step 4: Review and screen applicants

TermJobs supports resume parsing, candidate information extraction and matching against role requirements. Screening information, match scores and interview results help reviewers assess a candidate’s suitability.

These outputs are decision-support material. They do not guarantee candidate performance or replace a reviewer’s assessment.

### Step 5: Coordinate interviews

Teams can manage interview rounds, scheduling, invitations and evaluations. The application includes candidate and interviewer experiences, interview rooms and Google Calendar integration for supported scheduling workflows.

### Step 6: Prepare the engagement

Following selection, supported workflows connect candidates with work orders, agreement reviews, portal access and onboarding checklists. Candidates and workers can complete requested information and report onboarding issues.

### Step 7: Manage ongoing work

Contract professionals submit timesheets and expense claims through their portal. Hiring Managers review those submissions, while procurement, vendors and finance teams handle their assigned agreement and billing activities.

## 6. Main product capabilities

### Requisition management

Create and revise hiring requirements, track approval status, manage deadlines and retain the connection between a role and its candidates. Structured forms and conversational tools support different ways of working.

### Candidate screening and pipeline visibility

Collect applications and supplier submissions, review resumes and screening outputs, and follow candidates through supported hiring stages. Candidate details remain connected to the relevant requisition.

### Interviews and evaluations

Coordinate interview schedules, provide participants with the relevant access, and capture evaluations across interview rounds. Upcoming schedule views help reviewers see what needs attention next.

### Agreements, work orders and onboarding

Connect selection with engagement preparation. Work-order and agreement workflows give authorized teams a place to review documentation, while onboarding checklists track requested steps and reported issues.

### Timesheets, expenses and vendor billing

Support ongoing engagement administration through submitted hours, expense reviews and vendor billing workflows. Finance and operational reviewers can work from the information associated with the engagement.

### Notifications and collaboration

In-app notifications and team messaging help participants follow updates. The repository also includes Telegram, Zoho Cliq and Microsoft Teams integration modules. External integrations require the appropriate configuration and account connections; availability should be described accordingly on the public site.

## 7. The role of AI

TermJobs uses AI assistance to reduce repetitive preparation and help users work with hiring information. Examples include drafting requisitions, interpreting hiring requests, extracting resume information, presenting candidate matching insights and supporting conversational access to workflows.

The strongest public description is **AI-assisted hiring and workforce coordination**. Avoid promising that AI independently verifies every applicant, guarantees an unbiased decision or automatically delivers a successful hire.

## 8. Value for each side of the engagement

**For companies:** A shared view of hiring demand, approval responsibility, candidate progress and engagement administration.

**For Hiring Managers:** Less coordination across disconnected tools, with requirements, candidate information and relevant actions linked together.

**For Directors:** Clearer request ownership and a consistent place to review pending decisions and requested revisions.

**For staffing partners:** A structured way to receive requirements, submit profiles and follow associated engagement and billing activity.

**For candidates and contract professionals:** Dedicated experiences for applications, interviews, onboarding and ongoing workforce submissions.

These are intended product benefits. Quantified claims about hiring speed, cost reduction or placement success require supporting evidence before publication.
